📜 Rules

  1. You can not use custom presets, only original ones (exception: rule does not apply to prime difficulties)
  2. The modification must have a real author and verifier (exception: if there is no first video verification, then such things are used: Unconfirmed, unknown, first known).
  3. Modification should have an original download link
  4. Difficulty should be mentioned in the mod
  5. Modification will not be added if there are sexual items or 18+ content
  6. Texture packs would be in list if they make the game much harder (eg visually)
  7. Modification will be added after a week
  8. Modification cannot have adds (if it is on paintings, notes or as a secret then this rule will not work)
  9. Modification must be verified legitimately
  10. Challenges will not be added to List
  11. List dont including Additional options (except for the twins)
  12. Slenderina will not be added (Idk, probably in the future yes, but not sure)

🟡 Extended List: complex mods outside the main list.
