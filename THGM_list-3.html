THGM

Top Hardest Granny Mods Ever Verified

Community-driven project. Placements may be updated if new evidence or expert consensus appears.

I tried to make the list of the hardest modifications for Granny games as objective as possible and include every mod that exists. Thank you for using the list 😊

— Nachos 🌯

📜 Rules

  1. You cannot use custom presets, only original ones (exception: this rule does not apply to Prime difficulties).
  2. The modification must have a real author and verifier (exception: if there is no first video verification, labels such as Unconfirmed, Unknown, or First known may be used).
  3. The modification should have an original download link.
  4. The difficulty should be mentioned in the mod.
  5. Modifications will not be added if they contain sexual items or 18+ content (I'm a mod).
  6. Modifications will be added after a week.
  7. Modifications cannot have ads (if ads appear on paintings, notes, or as a secret, this rule does not apply).
  8. The modification must be verified legitimately.
  9. Challenges will not be added to the list.
  10. The list does not include additional options.

ℹ️ List information

🟢 Main List: fully confirmed mods.

🟡 Extended List: complex mods outside the main list.

🔴 Lost Media: mods for which walkthroughs or evidence have been partially or completely lost, including deleted videos, broken download links, or mods no longer available.
