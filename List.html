Community-driven project.

I tried to make the most objective list possible of the hardest Granny modifications and include every mod that exists.

Rules

  1. Levels MUST be verified legit.
  2. Mod would be placed after a week (and more).
  3. Mod must have an Author, real verifier, a verification video on YouTube or RUTube, and a working download link.
  4. No custom presets (this rule does not apply to mods in the top 4/5).
  5. No custom seed.
  6. Original video must be without montage.
  7. This list uses texture packs that make the game much more difficult.
  8. the modification must be mentioned in the mod

Legend

🟢 Main List: fully confirmed mods.
🟡 Extended List: complex mods outside the main list.
🔴 Lost Media: mods for which walkthroughs or evidence have been partially or completely lost.
